# 🚀 HireMate AI — B2B Sales Pitch & Feature One-Pager

## 📌 Executive Summary
**HireMate AI** is an enterprise-ready, white-label AI hiring readiness and mock interview platform designed for **Colleges, Coding Bootcamps, Placement Cells, and Recruitment Agencies**.

It bridges the gap between candidate resumes and job requirements by automating **skill gap analysis**, **STAR-format resume optimization**, and **real-time voice-enabled mock interviews**.

## 💡 Killer Commercial Features

1. **🎙️ Voice-Enabled AI Mock Interviews:**
   - Real-time Text-to-Speech question reading and Speech-to-Text mic recording.
   - Granular scoring on **Technical Accuracy**, **Communication**, and **Confidence**.

2. **⚡ 1-Click AI ATS Resume Optimizer:**
   - Ingests any JD and transforms candidate bullets using the proven **STAR method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
   - Injects target keywords to maximize ATS pass rates.

3. **🏢 100% White-Label B2B Branding:**
   - Buyers can customize platform name, logo, primary brand color, and PDF report header with 1 click.

4. **📊 1-Click Placement CSV & Email Dispatcher:**
   - Export student/candidate batches directly to Excel/CSV.
   - Auto-email professional PDF readiness reports to candidates or hiring managers.
