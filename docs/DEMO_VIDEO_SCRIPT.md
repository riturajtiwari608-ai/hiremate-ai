# 🎬 HireMate AI — 3-Minute Product Demo Video Script

**Target Audience:** Prospective SaaS Buyers, College Placement Directors, HR Tech Investors.  
**Video Goal:** Showcase the problem, the core AI features, and the white-label B2B value in under 180 seconds.

---

### [00:00 - 00:30] Hook & Problem Statement
* **Visual:** Screen opens on HireMate AI homepage with clean modern UI.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"Every year, millions of candidates fail job interviews not because they lack skills, but because their resumes don't pass ATS filters and they lack realistic mock interview practice. Meanwhile, placement teams and HR agencies are overwhelmed manually reviewing thousands of resumes. Meet **HireMate AI** — the all-in-one hiring readiness and voice-enabled interview simulation platform."*

---

### [00:30 - 01:15] Feature 1: Resume-JD Match & AI ATS Optimizer
* **Visual:** Log in as Candidate (`candidate@hiremate.ai`). Upload resume PDF, paste Job Description, click **Analyze**.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"Within seconds, HireMate AI breaks down the candidate's profile against the target job description. It calculates a precise match score, pinpoints missing vs matched technical skills, and generates a structured 7-day preparation roadmap.*
  > *With one click on **'AI 1-Click ATS Resume Optimizer'**, the system re-engineers candidate bullet points into measurable STAR-format achievements and injects target keywords to guarantee ATS pass readiness."*

---

### [01:15 - 02:00] Feature 2: Voice-Enabled AI Mock Interviewer
* **Visual:** Navigate to **AI Mock Interviews**. Start session. Click **"🔊 Listen to Question"**, then click **"🎙️ Answer with Voice (Mic)"** and speak.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"Next is our standout feature: the Voice AI Mock Interview Room. The AI speaks questions aloud, and candidates answer using their microphone in real time. Our AI evaluates each answer across three distinct pillars: Technical Accuracy, Communication Clarity, and Confidence — providing actionable feedback and improvement tips after every response."*

---

### [02:00 - 02:35] Feature 3: Admin & B2B White-Label Control
* **Visual:** Switch to Admin Panel (`admin@hiremate.ai`). Show White-label color picker, CSV Export, and Candidate Leaderboard.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"For colleges and recruitment agencies, HireMate AI is 100% white-label ready. Admins can update the platform name, logo, and theme colors in real time. They can track candidate placement readiness, export entire batches to CSV with one click, and email branded PDF reports directly to students and recruiters."*

---

### [02:35 - 03:00] Call to Action & Commercial Closing
* **Visual:** Show PDF Report download and the Pricing page.
* **Speaker:**
  > *"HireMate AI is fully production-ready, built with FastAPI, React, and Gemini AI. It is available for commercial acquisition and enterprise deployment today. Check out the link below for full source code access and live demo credentials."*
